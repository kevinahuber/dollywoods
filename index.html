Well, Hey There, Sugar!

Welcome to Elfwood, honey! This right here is the Internet's biggest ol' collection of Science Fiction and Fantasy artwork and writing, and I am just tickled pink you stopped by! Since 1996, we've been a home for creative folks from all over God's green earth to share their talents, get a little encouragement, and make friends who love all the same fantastical things they do.

Now darlin', whether you're here to wander through the enchanted galleries of Lothlorien, gaze at them futuristic visions over in Zone 47, curl up with a good tale in Wyvern's Library, or peek at what the fans have cooked up in the Fan Quarter — well, I just know you're gonna find somethin' that lights your fire. And honey, if you've got a talent hidin' inside you, don't be shy! Submit your own work — it costs nothin' but a little courage, and that's cheaper than a wig!

What's Been Goin' On, Y'all

July 15, 2002

Fan Quarter hits 500 galleries! Well, butter my biscuit! The Fan Quarter section just keeps growin' like kudzu in July since we opened it up last year. Thank y'all so much for all your beautiful fan artwork — keep it comin', darlin's!

July 8, 2002

Brand new FARP tutorials, honey! Three shiny new tutorials just landed in the Fantasy Art Resource Project: "Painting Dragon Scales," "Writing Believable Elven Cultures," and "Perspective for Fantasy Landscapes." Now that's what I call workin' 9 to 5 on your craft!

June 29, 2002

Server maintenance all done! We gave our servers a little nip and tuck — kinda like what I do before award season, except this actually makes things go faster! Page load times should be a whole lot better now. Thanks for puttin' up with us, sugar!

June 20, 2002

Elfwood Summer Art Challenge! This year's theme is "Midsummer's Dream," and I tell you what, that sounds prettier than a butterfly in a rhinestone factory. Submit your fantasy-themed summer artwork by August 31st. Winners get featured right here on the homepage for a whole month! Now wouldn't that just make your mama proud?

June 12, 2002

Comment guidelines updated. Now listen here, sugar — Elfwood is a community built on liftin' each other up. As I always say, if you want the rainbow, you gotta put up with a little rain, but you don't gotta be the rain! Be kind, be helpful, and remember that every artist started somewhere.

About This Here Elfwood

Elfwood is a non-commercial community run by volunteers with hearts bigger than their hard drives. All artwork and writing belongs to the folks who made it, period, end of story. We believe in growin' creativity through kind words and honest feedback — you know, the way my mama raised me back in the Smoky Mountains.

Submission rules: All artwork and stories gotta be your own original Science Fiction or Fantasy creations, honey. Fan art goes in the Fan Quarter. Please allow 1-2 weeks for our moderators to take a look — they're volunteers and they're doin' the Lord's work, bless their hearts.
