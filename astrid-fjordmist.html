Astrid Fjordmist's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Astrid Fjordmist
Location: Bergen, Norway
Member Since: June 1998
Favourite Media: Oils, Acrylic
Influences: Norse mythology, fjord landscapes, Scandinavian folk art

Hei! I paint creatures from Norse mythology set against the dramatic landscapes of my homeland. My studio overlooks a fjord, and the changing light inspires everything I create. I work primarily in oils and acrylics on large canvases, which I then photograph and scan for Elfwood.
