Elowen Mist's Writing Gallery
Name: Elowen Mist
Location: Cardiff, Wales, UK
Member Since: February 1998
Writes: Fantasy Fiction
Influences: Diana Wynne Jones, Terry Pratchett, Welsh mythology

I've been writing fantasy stories since I was ten and posting them on Elfwood since I was sixteen! My stories tend to be lighter in tone - I believe fantasy can be fun and warm without losing its sense of wonder. Welsh mythology features heavily in my work, along with a healthy dose of humour. Currently working on way too many stories at once!
