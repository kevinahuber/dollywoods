"Family on the Allegheny Ridge"
by Sasquatch Sam
Title: Family on the Allegheny Ridge
Artist: Sasquatch Sam
Date: Jun 18, 2002
Medium: Oils & Gouache
Description: Three figures — a big'un, a middle-sized one, and a little'un — silhouetted on a rocky ridge at sundown, the Allegheny Mountains rollin' out blue behind 'em. Based on a report from a turkey hunter up near Durbin who said he watched a family of 'em for ten minutes 'fore they melted into the trees like smoke.
