Brynn Ashfallow's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Brynn Ashfallow
Location: Vancouver, BC, Canada
Member Since: September 1999
Favourite Media: Watercolour, Coloured Pencil
Influences: Yoshitaka Amano, Art Nouveau, Pacific Northwest nature

Hi! I'm a graphic design student who spends way too much time painting fantasy creatures instead of doing my homework. My style mixes Art Nouveau elegance with Japanese illustration influences. I love painting dragons, phoenixes, and anything with elaborate wings or flowing hair.
