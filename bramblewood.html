Bramblewood's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Bramblewood
Location: Somerset, England, UK
Member Since: February 2002
Favourite Media: Pencil
Influences: Arthur Rackham, Beatrix Potter, English countryside

Hello! I'm Bramblewood (not my real name, obviously). I draw little woodland creatures in pencil - hedgehogs in armour, mice with swords, that sort of thing. Inspired by the fields and forests near my home in Somerset. Everything is done in graphite pencil on cartridge paper.
