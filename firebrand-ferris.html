Firebrand Ferris's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Firebrand Ferris
Location: Melbourne, Australia
Member Since: July 2000
Favourite Media: Digital, 3D Render
Influences: H.R. Giger, Syd Mead, Australian outback

G'day! I create fantasy and sci-fi crossover art using a mix of 2D digital painting and 3D renders in Bryce and Poser. I love blending organic fantasy creatures with mechanical elements. When I'm not making art, I'm studying computer science at uni. The Australian outback is surprisingly inspiring for alien landscapes!
