"Nessie in the Deep"
by Loch Lorna
Title: Nessie in the Deep
Artist: Loch Lorna
Date: Jul 5, 2002
Medium: Watercolour & Gouache
Description: A breathtaking underwater view of the Loch Ness Monster gliding through the dark peat-stained waters of the loch. Shafts of pale Scottish light filter down from above, illuminating a long-necked plesiosaur-like creature among submerged logs and ancient stone.
