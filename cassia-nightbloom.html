Cassia Nightbloom's Writing Gallery
Name: Cassia Nightbloom
Location: Montreal, Quebec, Canada
Member Since: January 2002
Writes: Science Fiction & Fantasy Fiction
Influences: Ursula K. Le Guin, Philip K. Dick, Margaret Atwood

Bonjour! I write stories that blur the line between science fiction and fantasy. What if magic was just technology we don't understand yet? What if the future looks more like the past than we expect? I explore these questions through short stories and novellas. New to Elfwood and absolutely loving this community!
