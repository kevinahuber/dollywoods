Lorna Deepwater's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Lorna Deepwater
Location: Burnsville, North Carolina, USA
Member Since: September 1999
Favourite Media: Watercolour, Gouache
Influences: Mountain lake reflections, Cherokee water spirit legends, foggy mornin's on the reservoir, my great-aunt Hazel's stories

I paint the things that live in the deep, dark waters of these mountains. Now honey, folks think you gotta go to Scotland for lake monsters, but we got our own right here in Appalachia. The Altamaha-ha down in Georgia, the Chessie in the Chesapeake, whatever's makin' them strange waves on Fontana Lake at night. My great-aunt Hazel, God rest her, she lived on the shore of Watauga Lake her whole life and she said the TVA flooded more than just towns when they built them dams — they trapped somethin' down there that was old when the Cherokee was young. I paint what she described.
