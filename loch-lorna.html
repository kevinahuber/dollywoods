Loch Lorna's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Loch Lorna
Location: Inverness, Scotland, UK
Member Since: September 1999
Favourite Media: Watercolour, Gouache
Influences: Scottish landscape tradition, marine biology illustration, sonar imagery, medieval sea monster maps

I live on the shores of Loch Ness (yes, really) and I paint lake monsters and sea serpents from folklore around the world. My neighbours think I'm daft, but when you've grown up watching the loch every single day, you start to see shapes in the water that don't match any fish in the book. My art imagines what lies beneath the surface of the world's most mysterious bodies of water.
