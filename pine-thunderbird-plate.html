"Cryptid Field Guide: Thunderbird"
by Jersey Pine
Title: Cryptid Field Guide: Thunderbird
Artist: Jersey Pine
Date: Jun 15, 2002
Medium: Ink & Watercolour
Description: A Victorian naturalist plate depicting the great Thunderbird in flight, wingspan measurements annotated in the margins. Below, a size comparison with a bald eagle and a human figure. Beautifully aged paper effect with coffee stains and field notes.
