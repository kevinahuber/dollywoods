Echo Nebula's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Echo Nebula
Location: San Francisco, CA, USA
Member Since: November 1999
Favourite Media: Mixed Media
Influences: Afrofuturism, Sun Ra, Octavia Butler, cosmic art

I create Afrofuturist science fiction art that imagines Black futures among the stars. My mixed media approach combines acrylic painting, collage, and digital elements. Inspired by Sun Ra, Octavia Butler, and the cosmic jazz tradition. San Francisco's diversity feeds my vision of inclusive futures.
