Well, howdy and welcome to the Fan Quarter, darlin'! This here's Elfwood's newest section, launched in 2001, and let me tell you, it's growin' faster than my hair on a good day! The Fan Quarter is home to fan-created artwork inspired by all the fantasy and sci-fi things we love — and honey, there's a whole lotta love in here. All art is non-commercial and created by fans with hearts full of passion! Currently hostin' 512 galleries with 3,847 artworks.

Now listen here, sugar: All fan art must be your own original creation (no tracin' or copyin' — that's like lip-syncin' and honey, I do NOT lip-sync!). The source material should be properly credited. Fan art is a celebration of the works we love, and we love a whole lot around here!
