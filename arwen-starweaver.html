Arwen Starweaver's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Arwen Starweaver
Location: Portland, Oregon, USA
Member Since: March 1999
Favourite Media: Pencil, Watercolour
Influences: Pre-Raphaelite art, Brian Froud, Celtic mythology

Hello there! I'm a self-taught artist who fell in love with fantasy art after reading The Lord of the Rings at age twelve. I specialize in ethereal portraits of elves and forest spirits, always trying to capture that magical twilight feeling. Currently studying art history at Portland State.
