Piney Combs's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Piney Combs
Location: Hinton, West Virginia, USA
Member Since: January 2001
Favourite Media: Ink, Watercolour
Influences: Old-timey naturalist books from the library, Audubon prints, my daddy's field notebooks, pressed flowers and specimen jars

Folks call me Piney on account of I'm always out in the pines sketchin'. I illustrate the cryptid critters of Appalachia like they was real animals in a naturalist's field guide — 'cause I reckon that's what they are. Things we ain't catalogued yet, is all. I use ink and watercolour on paper I tea-stain myself to look old-timey. Every creature gets proper Latin names, anatomical notes, and habitat descriptions, just like Mr. Audubon done with his birds. My daddy kept notebooks of every strange thing he saw in forty years of workin' for the Forest Service, and I'm turnin' his notes into art.
