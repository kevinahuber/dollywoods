"La Lechuza"
by Chupacabra Charlie
Title: La Lechuza
Artist: Chupacabra Charlie
Date: Jun 20, 2002
Medium: Acrylic
Description: A massive owl with the face of a woman perched on a power line above a lonely Texas highway at night. La Lechuza, the witch-owl of Mexican-American folklore, her wingspan blocking out the stars. A lone car's headlights approach in the distance.
