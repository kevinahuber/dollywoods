"Red Eyes Over the Holler"
by Mothman Molly
Title: Red Eyes Over the Holler
Artist: Mothman Molly
Date: Apr 15, 2002
Medium: Digital & Charcoal
Description: A panoramic view of a misty Appalachian valley at twilight. Two enormous red eyes glow from the ridgeline above a small cluster of houses. Every window in the holler is dark except one, where a figure stands watching.
