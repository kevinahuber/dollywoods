Favourite Media: Rhinestone-encrusted Watercolour, Glitter Digital (Photoshop 6.0 with extra sparkle)
Influences: The Smoky Mountains, butterflies, my mama's quilt patterns, and a whole lotta love

Well hey there, darlin'! Welcome to my little corner of Elfwood! I've been drawin' fantasy critters and sparkly things since I was knee-high to a grasshopper back in the hollers of Tennessee. Now I know what you're thinkin' — "Dolly, ain't you busy enough?" — but honey, it takes a lot of paint to look this magical! I mostly paint butterflies with rhinestones, dragons in big wigs, and enchanted Smoky Mountain landscapes. Comments and sweet talk are always welcome! And remember, it costs a whole lot of money to look this cheap, but art is FREE! :)
