Title: Cryptid Field Guide: Dover Demon
Artist: Jersey Pine

Medium: Ink
Description: A disturbing ink illustration of the Dover Demon as reported in 1977 Massachusetts sightings. The enormous head, glowing orange eyes, and spindly body rendered with clinical precision. Witness testimony excerpts handwritten around the border.
