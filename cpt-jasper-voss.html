Cpt. Jasper Voss's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Cpt. Jasper Voss
Location: Houston, Texas, USA
Member Since: May 1998
Favourite Media: Acrylic, Airbrush
Influences: NASA concept art, Ralph McQuarrie, retro-futurism

Retired Air Force, current space nut. I paint retro-futuristic scenes inspired by golden age sci-fi and NASA concept art. My studio is full of model rockets and vintage pulp magazines. I use traditional airbrush and acrylic because real artists don't need no stinkin' computer (just kidding, digital folks, y'all are great too).
