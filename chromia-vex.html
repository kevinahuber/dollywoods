Chromia Vex's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Chromia Vex
Location: Berlin, Germany
Member Since: August 2000
Favourite Media: Digital, Painter
Influences: Moebius, Philippe Druillet, European comics

Hallo! I create psychedelic science fiction art inspired by European comic traditions, especially French and Belgian bande dessinee. My work features alien landscapes, bizarre creatures, and surreal colour palettes. I use Corel Painter because it lets me get those organic brush textures I love.
