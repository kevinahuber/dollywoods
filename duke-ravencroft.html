Duke Ravencroft's Writing Gallery
Name: Duke Ravencroft
Location: Brighton, England, UK
Member Since: August 2000
Writes: Dark Fantasy Romance
Influences: Storm Constantine, Tanith Lee, Poppy Z. Brite, Gothic architecture

I write darkly romantic fantasy about complicated people in complicated situations, often involving sorcerers, demons, and morally ambiguous bargains sealed with more than a handshake. My stories explore power dynamics, obsession, and desire in settings where magic amplifies every emotion to its breaking point. Not for the faint of heart. Absolutely for the queer of heart.

Stories & Writing
The Demon's Pact Dark Romance UPDATED!
18,900 words — Chapters 1-6 (In Progress)
A disgraced sorcerer summons a demon to restore his power. The demon agrees — for a price that has nothing to do with souls and everything to do with surrender. A slow-burn descent into obsession between two beings who should absolutely not be trusted with each other.
Nights at the Shadow Court (Explicit) Gothic Erotica
12,400 words — Complete
In a decadent court of shadow fae, a mortal ambassador discovers that diplomacy takes many forms — some of which aren't in any protocol handbook. Five interconnected stories of intrigue, seduction, and glamour in a court where nothing is as it seems.
The Warlock's Price Fantasy Romance
7,200 words — Complete
Two rival warlocks, trapped together in a magical storm, discover that the tension between them was never really about professional jealousy. A enemies-to-lovers tale with actual fireballs and a very inconvenient truth potion.
