Influences: Terry Pratchett, Boccaccio's Decameron, Mozart's operas, bawdy medieval poetry

Guten Tag! I write humorous fantasy erotica because someone has to, and I happen to be very good at it. My stories are equal parts steamy and funny, set in a fantasy world that doesn't take itself too seriously. If Terry Pratchett wrote bodice-rippers, they might read something like my work (and I say that with the deepest respect and absolutely no authority). Currently writing the 'Misadventures' series about a hapless bard whose romantic escapades keep accidentally saving the kingdom.
