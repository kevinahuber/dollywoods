"The Hellhound of the Coal Camps"
by Shelly Hogtooth
Title: The Hellhound of the Coal Camps
Artist: Shelly Hogtooth
Date: Jun 10, 2002
Medium: Coloured Pencil
Description: In the old coal camp towns, miners told stories of a dog-like thing that glowed faint blue in the dark mine tunnels. Not a ghost, not a regular dog — somethin' in between. Eyes like hot coals, no sound when it walked, and it only appeared before a cave-in. Some said it was a warnin'. Some said it was the cause. I drew it in a tunnel mouth with a miner's lamp behind it, its shadow stretchin' long and wrong-shaped down the shaft.
