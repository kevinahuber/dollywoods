"Skunk Ape in the Cypress Swamp"
by Skunkape Shelly
Title: Skunk Ape in the Cypress Swamp
Artist: Skunkape Shelly
Date: Jul 2, 2002
Medium: Digital & Coloured Pencil
Description: Florida's own Bigfoot cousin — the foul-smelling Skunk Ape — wading through a cypress swamp at dawn. Spanish moss hangs from ancient trees, an alligator watches from a nearby bank, and the creature's matted orange-brown fur is rendered in incredible texture detail.
