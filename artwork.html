"Rhinestone Dragon at Sunset"
by Dolly Diamondfire
Rhinestone Dragon at Sunset
A magnificent purple dragon covered in rhinestones,
perched on a cliff above Dollywood, wearin' a big blonde wig
and spreadin' her bedazzled wings against a pink and gold
Tennessee sunset. She's fierce, fabulous, and breathin' glitter.
Title: Rhinestone Dragon at Sunset
Artist: Dolly Diamondfire
Date: July 12, 2002
Medium: Watercolour, Glitter, and Actual Rhinestones (Photoshop 6.0 for the sparkle effects because the real rhinestones don't scan too good)
Image Size: 800 x 600 pixels (142 KB — would be bigger but them rhinestones are heavy!)
Description: Well now, this here is my pride and joy, darlin'! I started with a traditional watercolour paintin' on the fancy paper I got from the art supply store in Knoxville, then I went and glued real rhinestones on the dragon's scales because if a dragon ain't sparkly, what's the point? Then I scanned it at 300dpi — which was a real adventure 'cause the rhinestones kept scratchin' the scanner glass, bless its heart — and did some extra sparkle effects in Photoshop.

This dragon is wearin' a big ol' blonde wig because I believe every creature deserves to feel glamorous, even fire-breathin' ones. She's sittin' on a cliff overlookin' what I imagine is a fantasy version of Dollywood, and instead of breathin' fire, she breathes glitter. The sunset is inspired by the real sunsets we get in the Smoky Mountains — Lord, they're so pretty they'll make you wanna slap your grandma (but don't do that, she's a nice lady).

I'm real proud of how the pink-to-gold gradient turned out. As I always say, find out who you are, and do it on purpose — and this dragon KNOWS who she is! Hope y'all like it! Comments, critique, and compliments all welcome. Especially compliments. :)
