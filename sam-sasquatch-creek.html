"Crossin' at Cranberry River"
by Sasquatch Sam
Title: Crossin' at Cranberry River
Artist: Sasquatch Sam
Date: Jul 10, 2002
Medium: Oils
Description: Based on my papaw's sightin'. A massive, shaggy figure mid-stride crossin' Cranberry River at first light, one enormous hand reachin' for a moss-covered boulder. Hemlocks and rhododendrons crowd the banks. The creature's half-hid in mornin' fog, just clear enough you can't pretend it's a bear. Papaw said it turned and looked at him, calm as Sunday, then just walked on into the laurel.
