"Field Guide Plate: The Greenbrier River Serpent"
by Piney Combs
Title: Field Guide Plate: The Greenbrier River Serpent
Artist: Piney Combs
Date: May 22, 2002
Medium: Ink & Watercolour
Description: Ain't many folks know about the Greenbrier River Serpent, but there's been sightin's goin' back to the 1800s. I drew it up proper: a long-necked critter about twenty feet, cross-section views of the neck, flipper detail, and hypothesized diet based on the river's fish population. Scientific as I could make it, which is pretty dang scientific if I say so myself.
