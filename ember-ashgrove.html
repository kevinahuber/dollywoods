Ember Ashgrove's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Ember Ashgrove
Location: Asheville, NC, USA
Member Since: May 2002
Favourite Media: Pencil, Charcoal
Influences: Brian Froud, Jim Henson, Appalachian folklore

Brand new to Elfwood! I just started sharing my art online and I'm so nervous! I draw fantasy creatures inspired by Appalachian folklore - think Jack Tales meets fairy tales. Everything is pencil and charcoal because I can't afford fancy art supplies yet (college student life!). Feedback welcome, please be gentle!
