Velvet Thornrose's Writing Gallery
Name: Velvet Thornrose
Location: Lewisburg, West Virginia, USA
Member Since: May 1999
Writes: Fantasy Romance & Erotica
Influences: Appalachian ghost stories, Anne Rice, granny witch traditions, moonshine, moth imagery

I'm a girl from the Greenbrier Valley who writes stories hotter than a coal stove in January. My specialty is Mothman romance — and before you laugh, honey, you ain't read my version. In my stories, the Mothman ain't just a harbinger of doom, he's a harbinger of doom and desire. I grew up hearin' my meemaw's stories about the strange things that happen in these mountains after dark, and I reckon the line between bein' scared and bein' excited is thinner than a spider's thread. Currently workin' on 'The Crimson Covenant,' a vampire saga set in the hollers.

Stories & Writing
The Crimson Covenant: Blood in the Holler Vampire Romance UPDATED!
34,200 words — Chapters 1-12 (In Progress)
When herbalist Jessamine follows a strange red light into the abandoned mine above Lewisburg, she stumbles into the court of a vampire lord who's been sleepin' under the Appalachian hills since before the first settlers crossed the mountains. He's ancient, he's dangerous, and he smells like cedar and old moonshine. A lush, steamy tale of forbidden desire set deep in the West Virginia hollers.
The Mothman's Bride (Explicit) Cryptid Erotica
8,600 words — Complete
On the anniversary of the Silver Bridge collapse, a woman in Point Pleasant sees those red eyes watchin' her from the TNT Area. But instead of runnin', she follows. What she finds ain't a monster — it's somethin' far more complicated, far more beautiful, and far more hungry. The Mothman ain't what you think he is, darlin'. Explicit content.
The Wampus Cat's Lover Fantasy Romance
5,100 words — Complete
A Cherokee woman cursed into the shape of a six-legged panther — the Wampus Cat — can only return to human form in the arms of someone who truly sees her. A lonely fiddle player in the Smokies hears somethin' howlin' harmony with his music. A tender, aching romance about seein' past the monster to the person underneath.
