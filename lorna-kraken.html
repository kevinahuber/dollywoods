"The Kraken Stirs"
by Loch Lorna
Title: The Kraken Stirs
Artist: Loch Lorna
Date: May 1, 2002
Medium: Gouache
Description: A massive kraken rising from the abyss beneath a tiny fishing vessel off the Norwegian coast. Tentacles the width of tree trunks curl around the hull. The scale is terrifying — most of the creature is still hidden in the black water below.
