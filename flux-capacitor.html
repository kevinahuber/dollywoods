Flux Capacitor's Fantasy Art Gallery
Name: Flux Capacitor
Location: Brisbane, Australia
Member Since: June 2002
Favourite Media: Digital, Bryce 3D
Influences: Roger Dean, 70s prog rock album art, coral reefs

Brand spanking new to Elfwood! I make alien landscapes in Bryce 3D and then paint over them in Photoshop. My style is heavily influenced by Roger Dean's Yes album covers - floating islands, impossible rock formations, alien skies. The Great Barrier Reef also inspires a lot of my organic alien world designs.
